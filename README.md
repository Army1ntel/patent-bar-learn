# Patent Bar Learn

A free, offline study tool for the USPTO registration examination — the patent bar.

**Live app:** https://patentbar.ernestsumrall.com

- **24 modules, 169 questions, 103 glossary terms**
- Five answer choices per question, matching the real exam format
- Every answer carries its citation to the MPEP, 35 U.S.C., or 37 CFR
- Confusing terms are marked inline with a reference numeral — tap for a plain-English definition
- Spaced repetition: three correct in a row masters a question; miss a mastered one and it returns
- Weak-spot drills, a 20-question mixed drill, and a 50-question half session at exam pace
- Works offline. Installs to a phone Home Screen. No account, no tracking, no server — progress stays in your browser

## Coverage

**Track I — Foundations:** the exam itself · who may practice · duty of disclosure and the IDS · fees, entity status, petitions · inventorship, ownership, assignment · professional conduct

**Track II — Filing and Patentability:** getting a filing date · priority and benefit · AIA 102 · pre-AIA 102 and swearing behind · 103 obviousness · 112 · 101 · claim drafting and construction

**Track III — Prosecution and Post-Issuance:** replies and abandonment · amendment practice · after final, RCE, appeal · restriction and double patenting · publication and third parties · reissue and reexamination · patent term · PCT

**Track IV:** design patents · plant patents

## Source materials

Built from the published source materials for the registration examination: the MPEP, Ninth Edition, Revision 01.2024; the Consolidated Trial Practice Guide (November 2019); the Changes to Representation of Others Before the USPTO final rules; and the Global/IP5 Patent Prosecution Highway notice.

**Check the version stamp in the app header before you study.** When the USPTO moves to a new MPEP revision, some answers here will go stale.

## Install it on your phone

Open the link in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android), then Share → Add to Home Screen. It runs offline from there.

## Found an error?

Please open an issue. Include the module, the question, and the MPEP or CFR section you believe is correct. Corrections are the most valuable contribution you can make to this.
